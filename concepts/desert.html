05 · Desert Island · Contact

Already good. Now make it true.

Desert works, so this isn't a redesign. It's eight upgrades that make the panel feel alive and get more people to actually press send. All of them are working in the demo below.

Where it's at

src/islands/contact.js: a desert-sunset aside with socials and status pills, plus a Web3Forms message form. Solid and clean.

What's off

The sunset is always a sunset, so it's just decoration. Everyone gets the same blank form whether they're hiring or saying hi. There's no résumé anywhere, the one thing recruiters hunt for.

The move

Make the scene true, the form smart, the résumé one click away. The sky shows your real time in London. The form asks what it's about first.

The amplified panel

Try it, it's live

Drag the time slider to see the sky over a day. Pick an intent and watch the form change. Copy the email. Hit send with Ctrl + Enter. Nothing actually sends.

My pick

Ship 01 to 06 now. 07 is the cherry.

The résumé button and intent chips are the two that actually change outcomes: more recruiters reach you and the messages you get are better. Copy email, local time and keyboard polish are an afternoon. The true sky is what makes Desert feel like a place instead of a form, and it costs about 60 lines.

The paper plane is the delight moment, add it once the rest is in. Also drop Instagram from the contact list if you want Desert to read purely professional, and keep "Replies in about a day" only if it stays true.
